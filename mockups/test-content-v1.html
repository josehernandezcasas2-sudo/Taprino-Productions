Four questions

  1. What's ticked when the page opens? Placeholders and titles with a deletion request ticked; anything that only says test left unticked, so a real film with “test” in its name can't go by accident.Recommended as drawn
  2. Who can use it? Full admins only — sub-admins don't see it.Recommended: full admins only
  3. The old “Remove placeholder content” button in Settings? Move removal here; Settings keeps “Generate placeholders”.Recommended: move it here
