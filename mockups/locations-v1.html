Locations — v1 mockups

A new kind of account on the Connect side: a host lists a place to shoot in. It sits beside Crew Call: same account, same Messages, same track record idea, same admin review before anything goes live. Anyone signed in can host (like a working card); a listing is approved by admin before it's public (like a pitch). Six screens below, then the phone, then the list of things to decide.

Money. A host sets an hourly rate, a day rate, or "make a deal" (the producer proposes a number). Where Crew Call moves no money, locations can: the request becomes a Stripe Checkout once the host accepts, paid through the same Connect setup creators already use for backing, with the platform cut and the deposit on top. That's a decision, not a given — see the questions at the bottom.

7 · Things you may not have thought of

in the mockups above, or easy to add

  • Address stays private until the host accepts — the listing shows the neighbourhood and distance, the map pin is fuzzy, the exact address, gate code and host phone unlock on confirmation.
  • Deposit held, not charged. Stripe can authorise the deposit and release it after “did it go OK?”, so the host is covered without the producer losing the money for weeks.
  • Overtime added by the host, approved by the producer in the thread, charged to the card on file. No awkward wrap-time negotiation.
  • Counter-offers. “Make a deal” needs a counter, and a rate listing benefits from one too (a student asking $250 on a $600 day gets a $400 counter instead of a no).
  • 48-hour answer window and a reply-time number on the host card, so producers know who actually responds. Three expiries hide the listing until the host checks in.
  • “Shot here” credits link a listing to the Snippets series, pitch or series that used it. That’s the host’s track record (Hosted 6), and it’s free marketing for both.
  • Light and sound facts scouts actually ask: which way the windows face, best hour, power tie-in or household circuits, flight path / street noise, ceiling height, load-in width.
  • Walkthrough video and floor plan through the uploads that already exist (Cloudflare for video, Storage for the PDF, like Film University files).
  • Load-in and wrap photos from both sides in the request thread: the simplest damage dispute prevention there is.
  • Host sets one of three cancellation policies (flexible / moderate / strict) instead of free text, so refunds are automatic.
  • Saved places and a scout board: save listings per project, share the shortlist with the crew (reuses the My List / wishlist pattern).
  • Attach a location to a Crew Call post (“shooting at the bungalow, Oct 24”) and to a pitch’s “Crew needed” block, so a project’s shoot days hang together.
  • Verified host = ID plus proof they can let a crew in (deed, lease, or a letter) — reviewed in the admin queue beside pitches and ads. Unverified hosts can still list, but the badge is what producers filter on.
  • Date alerts: “tell me when something like this opens Oct 24” for producers; “someone searched your area for Oct 24” for hosts with a blocked day.
  • Permit help as a listing fact (host handles / host can help / you handle), since in LA the permit is often the hard part, not the house.
  • House rules as structured fields (hours, curfew, crew max, pets, smoking, furniture) plus one free-text paragraph, so the rules show on the request and the day sheet.
8 · Decisions for you

before building

  • Money through the site or not? Crew Call moves no money. The mockups assume locations do, through Stripe Connect like backing (platform cut + deposit hold). The alternative is “arrange it yourselves” with no cut and no deposit protection.
  • Platform cut. Peerspace takes 15% from hosts + a 5% service fee from the booker; Giggster is similar. The mock shows 10% from the host, nothing from the producer.
  • Who can host? Anyone signed in (like a working card), or only after admin verification? The mock: anyone can create, admin approves before it’s public.
  • Name. “Locations” under Connect with a “host” is what the mocks use; “Location Manager” reads as a job title (the crew role) rather than the owner of a place. Your call.
  • Hours vs days. Keep both plus “make a deal” (as mocked), or simplify to day rate + deal?
  • Instant book? Some hosts will want “book at my rate without asking”. Easy to add later as a per-listing toggle; left out of v1.
