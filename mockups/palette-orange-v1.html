Orange palette, v1: the pink becomes orange, and what has to move with it

Every colour on the site and in the app comes from one list of tokens (styles/globals.css :root, mirrored in mobile/src/lib/theme.js). The pink is --brass: the logo's "Tapa", headings, the active tab, the hot chips, message bubbles, booked days. Changing it means changing the warm colours around it too, or the site reads as three oranges fighting. Below: the same screens under the current palette and two orange candidates, the swatch tables with contrast checked, and the list of what changes in the code.

The conflicts an orange primary creates: the secondary accent (--olive, really an amber) sits right next to it on the wheel; the danger colour is a burnt orange; the warning colour is an orange; the "rust" content tag is orange-brown. The proposal moves the secondary to a gold, danger to a true red, warning to a deeper gold, and keeps rust (it's muted enough and only tags vertical video). Blues, mint and the text stay exactly as they are.

3. What changes in the code

one commit, web + app
Website
  • styles/globals.css: the :root values, and the 47 hard-coded pink tints (rgba(248,95,115,…)) become rgba(var(--brass-rgb),…) so they follow the token from now on.
  • lib/themeColors.js: the defaults the Styles & Guides theme editor compares against. Any override saved there in the past wins over the new defaults, so after the switch that page should show every swatch as "default"; if one is marked changed, press Reset.
  • Inline literals in 8 files (pitch/episode/ad forms' notice boxes, the schedule legend, two admin pages, the account page's age notice, the crew avatar colours) move to the token.
  • lib/profileBanner.js: the banner colour choice "Tropical pink" becomes the orange; a profile that already picked pink keeps its saved value.
  • lib/university.js: the course cover gradients named by token.
  • lib/messages.js: the email's quote border.
  • public/og-image.png (the link preview card) is regenerated; logo.svg / icon.svg don't use the pink.
App and elsewhere
  • mobile/src/lib/theme.js: the same tokens, plus a brassTint(alpha) helper replacing the 14 literal tints in screens (university chips, the elevator heart glow, live tags, booked days, the drop-up press state, message bubbles, the numbers chart, the age notice).
  • The Figma design system's variables are ported by hand (no live sync).
  • Mockups in mockups/ keep their pink; they're history.
  • Nothing in the database changes. A member's saved pink banner stays pink until they pick again.
