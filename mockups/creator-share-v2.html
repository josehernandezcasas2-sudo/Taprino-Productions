Creator Share v2 — the money lanes, the creator's home base, referrals, tips, and what to do in Stripe

v1 designed the member's side: a slice of every paid membership that the member points at up to 10 creators, with a default until they do. v2 is the whole loop around it. Creators bring their audience → fans watch, subscribe, tip and back projects → money flows back to creators → the library grows → it attracts new viewers and creators. It only turns if a creator has a reason to send fans to their Studio Tapa TV link instead of YouTube or Patreon, so v2 adds the four things that give them one, keeps the money lanes apart from the start, and ends with the exact list of what has to happen in Stripe.

The one rule that keeps it legal and auditable: a ledger line belongs to exactly one lane, and only Earnings moves money by transfer. 3. Referral attribution — the mechanic that makes creators bring people

earnings lane · the "bigger cut"

A fan who subscribes through a creator's link earns that creator a referral bonus on every renewal, for as long as that subscription lives — on top of the member share, and untouched by what the member later does with their lineup. The referred member also starts with the referrer as their first pick, so the share goes to them too until the member changes it.

1 · Click/@mara or ?via=mara on any page sets a via cookie: 30 days, last click wins.middleware.js
2 · JoinThey sign up and pick a plan. Checkout carries client_reference_id = the member and subscription_data.metadata.via = Mara./api/create-checkout-session
3 · LockThe first paid invoice writes member_referrals: member, creator, subscription, locked_at. Self-referral refused. Nothing locks on a $0 or trial invoice.invoice.paid
4 · EarnEvery invoice.paid on that subscription adds a ledger line for Mara: $1.00 Standard · $2.00 Premium · $1.00 Sponsor (flat per tier, admin setting, locked per payment).creator_ledger · kind = referral
5 · EndThe subscription is cancelled → the attribution ends. Resubscribe within 90 days keeps it; after that the next link wins.customer.subscription.deleted

Optional · creator codes
A reason for the fan, a second attribution path

One Stripe coupon ("Creator welcome", say $2 off the first month) and a promotion code per creator — MARA — that the site creates through the API, first-time members only. A code on the invoice attributes the member even when the cookie is gone (typed on a phone, a different browser). Checkout already has allow_promotion_codes on.

Not the same thing as the existing promo codes (comped access, migration 041); those stay as they are. Whether fans get a discount at all is your call (§10).
  • Who the referrer is is never shown to the member; it just shows up as "You joined through Mara's link" on their lineup card, with Mara pre-filled and removable.
  • Guards: no self-referral, no bonus on $0 invoices, one attribution per subscription, an admin can void one (fraud, a creator removed). Referred members count on the creator's Money page and in Your Numbers.
  • Why flat, not a percentage: "Bring a Standard member, earn $1 a month for as long as they stay" is a sentence a creator will repeat. A percentage of net is a spreadsheet.

7. The plumbing — ledger, batch, edge cases

migration 088 + lib/creatorLedger.js
Tables
  • member_lineups — member, creator, weight, position; ≤ 10 per member, member ≠ creator.
  • member_customers — Stripe customer id → Clerk user. Today the link only goes the other way (the Clerk user carries stripeCustomerId); an invoice.paid needs the reverse, so we write this row when a customer is linked and stamp metadata.clerkUserId on the Stripe customer too.
  • member_payments — one per paid invoice: member, subscription, tier, amount, share, bonus, period, basis used. Unique on the invoice id.
  • member_referrals — member, creator, subscription, locked_at, ended_at, source (link / code), voided_by.
  • creator_ledger — creator, lane (earnings / tips / backing), kind (member_share / referral / ad_share / tip / backing / reversal / payout), signed cents, the source ids, created_at. Tips and backing lines are mirrors for the Money page (moves_money = false).
  • creator_payouts — creator, cents, period, stripe_transfer_id, status, the ledger ids it covered.
  • creator_tips — like pitch_donations: amount, cut, fee cover, status, note, visible.
  • site_settings — share and bonus per tier, tip cut, payout minimum, the referral window and grace days.
Behaviour
  • Split maths is pure and tested (lib/creatorShareRules.js, like donationMath and adPlanRules): cents never vanish in rounding — the remainder goes to the first pick.
  • The batch (Vercel cron, the 1st, 09:00 UTC; "run now" + dry run on the admin page): for each creator with a ready account and ≥ $10 unpaid earnings, one transfers.create with transfer_group = the period and the ledger ids in metadata; a payout line closes them. It checks balance.retrieve() first and pays what the available balance covers, oldest first; the rest waits for the next run and the admin page says so.
  • Refund or dispute on a membership payment: its share and bonus lines get a reversal if still unpaid; if already transferred, the studio absorbs it (same posture as Pitch Room). charge.dispute.created is a new webhook event.
  • Idempotent through stripe_events; a replayed invoice.paid never splits twice (unique invoice id).
  • US creators only in v1. Transfers from a US platform to a non-US account need Stripe's cross-border payouts and a different agreement; the Express onboarding already limits who gets an account.
  • Audit log entries for every batch, void, refund and settings change. The admin page: /admin/creator-money (Pitch Room group): ledger by month, who's owed what, held balances, the batch and its history, referrals to review, tips refunds, settings.

9. Terms — v1's drafts stand; these are the additions

numbers bracketed

Section 5 · if you submit work — after "Member share"

Referral bonus. When someone subscribes through your creator link or code, we credit you $1.00 (Standard, Sponsor) or $2.00 (Premium) for each renewal of that subscription while it stays active, on top of any member share. A subscription is attributed once, to the last link or code used in the 30 days before joining; it ends when the subscription is cancelled, unless it is resumed within 90 days. You may not refer yourself, pay people to subscribe, or run ads that misrepresent Studio Tapa TV; we can void an attribution that breaks this. When you share your link or code, say that you earn from it. Bonuses are paid with your member share, under the same schedule and minimum.

Advertising revenue. [still to be decided; the ledger is built to take it when it is]

New section · Tips

For fans. You can tip a creator from their profile or a title's page. A tip is a thank-you, not a purchase: it buys nothing and isn't a donation to a charity. Of each tip, Studio Tapa TV keeps 5% and you cover the card processing fee shown before you pay; the rest goes to the creator's account at Stripe. Tips are final except where the law requires a refund or we decide one is warranted — email contact address. Notes with tips are moderated; be kind.

For creators. Tips reach your connected Stripe account at the moment they're paid, less our cut and the processing fee the fan covered. You're responsible for your own taxes on tips, member share, bonuses and backing; Stripe issues the forms where the law requires it. Payouts need a Stripe account holder who is 18 or older.

Section 4 addition. The "Your creator share" paragraph from v1, plus one sentence: "If you joined through a creator's link, they're your first pick until you change it, and they receive a referral bonus from us that doesn't come out of your share."
