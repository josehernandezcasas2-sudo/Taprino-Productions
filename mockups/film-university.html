Scenes that turn

Every scene is a small argument: someone wants something, and by the end the situation has changed. We look at three scenes from Studio Tapa shorts and mark the turn in each.

Exercise · 20 min

Find the turn

Take any scene you've written. Write one sentence for the start state and one for the end state. If they're the same, the scene isn't done. Rewrite until they differ.

Attached document

Scene beat worksheet

One page you fill in per scene.
