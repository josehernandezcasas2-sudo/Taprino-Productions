Notes

  • Every number comes from Stripe when the page opens: the creator's balance, their payout schedule, upcoming and recent payouts, and the last four digits of their bank account. Nothing new is stored on our side.
  • "Next payout" is Stripe's own upcoming payout when one exists. Otherwise it's the available balance plus the date their schedule says it goes out, so it's labelled an estimate.
  • A failed payout (bank rejected it) shows a red Failed pill and a line telling them to fix their bank in Stripe.
  • The "Get paid" card on /creator/pitch/dashboard stays and gets the same amounts, so the two places never disagree.
