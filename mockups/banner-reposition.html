MOCKUP · account › banner · upload + reposition

1. Banner editor, at rest

The upload control becomes two plain pills, Upload photo and Remove photo, with no file-picker box. A new Reposition pill appears once there's a photo. Colour swatches stay as they are. (The visible "Choose File" box in your screenshot was a bug and is fixed separately.)

J

A colour shows when there's no photo, and behind a photo while it loads.

2. Repositioning

Tap Reposition and the preview becomes draggable: pull the photo up or down (and sideways if it's wider than the frame) until the part you want is in view. A dashed line marks where the avatar overlaps, so you don't hide a face under it. Done keeps it; Save profile stores it. On touch screens it's a one-finger drag. Below the preview a slider does the same thing for keyboards and screen readers.

3. What visitors see

The public profile hero uses the saved position, on the website and in the app, so the same slice of the photo shows everywhere. Nothing is re-cropped or re-uploaded; the position is just a pair of percentages stored with the profile.

Same editor on the Account screen: Upload, Reposition and Remove under the preview; in Reposition the preview is draggable with one finger, Done saves straight away (the app saves the banner on its own, as it does now).

How it's stored

One new columnuser_profiles.banner_position, e.g. 50% 62% (migration 084). Blank means centred, which is what every existing banner gets, so nothing moves until someone adjusts it.
No new imagesThe photo stays exactly as uploaded. The website applies the position as background-position; the app offsets the image inside its frame the same way.
