Film University — as a university

Rethought from the Workshop. The unit people enrol in is a course: a short, ordered programme inside a department (Directing, Writing, …). Each course has a syllabus of lessons (video or reading), course materials to download (templates, worksheets, sample files), and optional assignments. A Library page collects every downloadable file across courses. Progress is per device until sign-in sync exists. Four screens below plus the admin.

Scenes that turn

Every scene is a small argument: someone wants something, and by the end the situation has changed. We look at three scenes from Studio Tapa shorts and mark the turn in each.

Assignment · 20 min

Find the turn

Take any scene you've written. One sentence for where it starts, one for where it ends. If they match, rewrite until they differ. Use the beat worksheet.

Admin — /admin/university
