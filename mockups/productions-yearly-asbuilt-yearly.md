# Yearly Stream plans — as built (part A of `productions-yearly-contract.md`, 2026-11-19)

Server, website and app. No migration of its own (127 was written; nothing in it needed changing — see the end). Nothing committed.

## What it does

- Each Stream tier (Standard, Premium, Sponsor) may have a second live recurring price with `interval: 'year'` in Stripe; the plans now carry `monthly` and `yearly` (`{ priceId, amountCents }` or null) beside the flat fields, which stay the monthly price for older app builds. Nothing is created by code.
- Checkout takes `interval: 'month' | 'year'` (month by default) and sells that price; a billing it doesn't know → 400 "Pick monthly or yearly."; a tier with no yearly price → 400 "Sponsor has no yearly price yet — pick monthly, or another plan."
- The picker (website and app) shows a Monthly / Yearly switch only when some tier has a yearly price. On Yearly a card reads "$79 a year" and "$6.58 a month · 2 months free", the button "Join Standard · yearly" / "Become a Sponsor · yearly" and checks out yearly; a tier with no yearly price is greyed, "Monthly only for now.", its button off. A member's plan row and the hub's tile read "$79 a year" and "Renews <date> · yearly" (or "· monthly").
- The creator share, month by month: a yearly invoice records `months 12, months_credited 1, next_month_at` = paid_at + 1 calendar month and credits month 1 exactly as a monthly invoice does (picks → watching → pool, plus the referral bonus in its window). The daily cron step `creditYearlyMonths()` credits each later month on its date, split by the member's picks **that day**, the bonus by `referralEarnsBonus` on that month's date. A refund (full or partial) or a dispute stops the months; full refund and dispute also reverse the unpaid credits; a won dispute gives the credits back and does not restart the months. A cancelled-at-period-end plan keeps crediting through the paid year.
- **Switching in the portal never pays two shares for one month** (the coordinator's follow-up of the same day, replacing my first item 2): monthly → yearly records the year's month 1 as credited without writing its lines when the member's monthly payment already credited that month, and the cron credits the 11 after it; yearly → monthly stops the running year (`stopped_at`, nothing next, "switched to monthly") before a monthly invoice that credits a month is recorded.
- Studio money keeps the share still to credit on running yearly payments back from the sweep (`yearlySharesAheadCents`), shows it as its own line, freezes it into a sweep's breakdown, and takes its change since the last sweep out of the membership margin.
- Artist Support on a yearly plan: "Your share goes out month by month. Change your picks any time; the next month follows them." and the picks line says "changes apply from next month".
- Terms sections 4 and 5 (monthly or yearly, ten months' price, credited month by month, a refunded or disputed year credits no further months), `TEXT_UPDATED` 2026-11-19; three questions added to `legal/creator-share-terms-draft.md` (§8).

## Decisions I made that Jose may want to change

1. **A later month's amount is the share recorded at payment** (`share_cents` / `bonus_cents` on the row), not the Creator Share numbers on that month's date. A year is paid at one price, Studio money holds back exactly that, and the Terms say a credit never changes; the flip side is that a share raise (or a cut, after its 30 days' notice) reaches a yearly member's remaining months only at renewal. Say if it should follow the numbers month by month.
2. **Plan switches through the portal** (the coordinator's fix, replacing what I first built, which let both paths credit the same month):
   - *Monthly → yearly*: when the year starts inside the calendar month the member's latest monthly payment paid for (that payment still paid, with a share; same member, any subscription), the year's month 1 is recorded as credited (`months_credited 1`) with no lines, and the cron credits months 2–12. Across the switch the member's creators get one share per month (the monthly one for the switch month, then 11 from the year — 12 shares for a little over 12 months paid).
   - *Yearly → monthly*: a monthly invoice that credits a month stops the member's running years first. **One deviation from the message's wording**: only an invoice that actually credits a month stops the year. Stripe bills a switch to monthly by turning the unused year into credit and paying the monthly invoices from it ($0 `amount_paid`, which credit nothing); stopping the year at the switch would leave those months — paid for with the year's money — crediting nobody. So the year keeps crediting while the credit pays the monthly invoices, and stops the moment a monthly invoice pays (and credits) a month. If you meant the switch itself to stop it, it's one condition in `monthlyStopsYear` (drop `creditsMonth`). At the hand-over the year's last month and the first paid monthly month can still overlap by part of a month (shares are per payment, not per day).
   - The two marks live in `member_payments.reverse_reason` on a still-paid row ("month 1 paid by the monthly plan", "switched to monthly") — 127 has no column for a note, and nothing reads `reverse_reason` on a paid row (Studio money and the dispute restore read it on reversed rows only; a later refund overwrites it with the refund's reason, by which time the year is stopped anyway). A `note` column in a later migration would be cleaner.
   - Edge left: a monthly invoice dated *before* a year (delivered late by Stripe's retries or the daily catch-up) never stops that year (`paid_at` guard), and if it lands after the year was recorded with its own month 1, that one month is credited twice. Real deliveries come in order within seconds; the catch-up's late ones are rare.
   - Same-interval switches (Standard yearly → Premium yearly) are prorations only: nothing new is credited and the year keeps its Standard share; the year is never stopped by them.
3. **A discounted year** (a promotion code) credits each month against a twelfth of what was paid, so creators never get more than the member paid in total.
4. **The bonus in later months** needs month 1 to have earned one (the amount is month 1's), the referral still active (a deleted subscription ends it), inside its window on that month's date, and the referrer not a leaver.
5. **"Yearly · 2 months free"** promises the smallest saving of any tier that has both prices (all three are 2 at Jose's prices).
6. **The cron's yearly step gets 20 s and at most 300 months a run** (the catch-up has 25 s in the same function, and the route sets no `maxDuration`); the rest wait for the next day. With yearly payments spread over the month that is plenty for now; a big launch day would take a few days to catch up.
7. **The margin on /admin/money** takes the change in "still to credit" since the last sweep (as ads served does with advertisers' credit), so a year sold doesn't read as the studio's and later months don't read as losses. Not asked for; small; say if it should go.
8. The Money page lists a later month's line as an ordinary "Member share" line; the month note is kept on the line but not shown.
9. `lib/membershipTiers.js` is unchanged: the billing words live in `lib/yearlyPlanRules.js`, which the app copies word for word (membershipTiers has no word-for-word app copy).
