Creator Share — members decide where their share goes

A slice of every paid membership is set aside for creators. The member names up to 10 creators (their lineup) and that slice is split between them on every renewal. A member who never picks anyone still funds creators: a default rule spends their share for them until they choose. Nothing here is built; this page is the proposal to sign off before any code.

Do the Terms already have this in mind? No. Section 4 (membership) only promises access, Section 5's Revenue paragraph is still the bracketed "to be finalised" placeholder, and the About page says ads "help maintain the site and fund new projects". Nothing forbids it either — the placeholder is exactly where these words belong. Section 9 of this page drafts them.

1. The share, per tier

numbers to decide

A flat amount per tier, set on /admin/settings and locked into each payment the moment it lands (a later change never moves old money). Flat beats a percentage because it's a sentence a member understands: "$2 of your $7.99 goes to your creators."

3. The default — what happens until they pick

rule to decide

Jose's rule: not setting it up still funds creators. Proposal: the default is personal, not a house pick — it follows the member's own watching, so two members who never touch the card still fund different people.

1 · LINEUP SET

Everything goes to the picks. Three creators picked = the whole share split three ways, not 3/10ths. A half-empty lineup is still a full choice.

2 · LINEUP EMPTY

Follows what you watched. The 30 days before the renewal, from watch_history: each creator's share = their titles' plays ÷ all plays. Watch minutes once we save progress; plays until then.

3 · WATCHED NOTHING

The site-wide pool. Split between every eligible creator by their share of the month's views (creator_views in Redis already counts this per day).

  • Alternatives if you'd rather: straight to the pool (simplest, least personal) · Studio Tapa's picks of the month (editorial, needs curating) · a 50/50 of watched + pool.
  • Eligible creator = has at least one released title on the site. Pitch-only creators? Location hosts? Proposed no — this is for the people whose work members are watching; Pitch Room already funds pitches.
  • Podcast and Snippet creators count the same as film and series creators. Studio Tapa's own titles: in the pool or out? (open — if in, the studio funds itself from the default.)

6. The money — how it actually moves

stripe connect transfers

Membership payments land in the studio's own Stripe balance (a normal subscription, not a destination charge like Pitch Room). So creators are paid by Stripe Connect transfers from that balance into the same Express account Pitch Room and Locations already pay into (creator_payout_accounts; the transfers capability is already requested when the account is created).

1 · RenewalStripe fires invoice.paid (we don't listen for it yet). We record the payment: member, tier, amount, share, period.member_payments
2 · SplitThe share is split by the lineup as it stands right then (or the default rule) into one ledger line per creator.creator_share_ledger · basis = picked / watched / pool
3 · AccrueLines add up per creator through the month. The creator card shows the running total; the member's history shows the split.nothing moves yet
4 · Pay · 1st of the monthOne transfer per creator with a ready account and ≥ $10 accrued. Under $10 carries over. No account → it waits.stripe.transfers.create · creator_share_payouts
5 · BankStripe pays the creator's bank on their Express schedule. Their Payouts card already shows that part.existing PayoutsCard
  • Why a $10 minimum: Stripe's Express pricing (US) is $2 per active connected account per month plus 0.25% + 25¢ per payout. Paying $0.60 to a creator would cost more than it sends. Accruing to $10 keeps the fees sane — and the Terms placeholder asks for exactly this threshold and schedule.
  • Refunded membership payment (charge.refunded, which we already handle for pitches and bookings): its ledger lines are reversed if not yet paid out; if they were, the studio absorbs it. Same posture as Pitch Room refunds.
  • Idempotent through stripe_events (migration 083) like every other webhook; a replayed invoice.paid never splits twice.
  • The batch runs from a Vercel cron on the 1st with a "run now" button and a dry-run preview on the admin page; every transfer carries the ledger ids in its metadata so Stripe and the database can be reconciled.
  • Admin page /admin/creator-share (Pitch Room group, cap manage_payouts or full admins): this month's ledger, who's owed what, held balances (no account), the payout batch and its history, settings per tier. The existing Payments checklist on /admin/pitch-room gains a row: "invoice.paid is on the account webhook".

9. Terms — draft words

needs your sign-off, numbers bracketed

Section 4 · membership — new paragraph

Your creator share. Part of every paid membership is set aside for the people who make what's on Studio Tapa TV: $2.00 a month on Standard and $4.00 on Premium ($2.00 on Sponsor). You decide where it goes by naming up to 10 creators on your account page; it's split between them, equally unless you set your own shares, each time your membership renews. If you haven't named anyone, it follows what you watched in the 30 days before the renewal, and if you watched nothing it's shared among all creators on the platform in proportion to how much their work was watched. Changing your list affects future renewals only. This is part of your membership fee, not a donation or a purchase from the creator, and it doesn't give you anything beyond the membership itself.

Section 5 · if you submit work — replaces the Revenue placeholder

Member share. When a member names you, or watches your released work under the default described in section 4, a share of that member's payment is credited to you. Credits are paid on the 1st of each month through Stripe to the connected account you set up on your account page, once your balance reaches $10; smaller balances carry over. Credit you haven't set up an account to receive within 12 months returns to the platform pool. If a member's payment is refunded or reversed, the matching credit is reversed too. We publish the per-tier amounts and may change them with 30 days' notice; the share already credited to you never changes.

Advertising revenue. [still to be decided — ad revenue share, if any, is a separate decision from this one]

  • The About page's line ("ads help maintain the site and fund new projects") gains a sentence: "Members go further: part of every membership goes straight to the creators they choose."
  • The tier picker (lib/membershipTiers.js perks, web + app) gets the share as a perk line on Standard/Premium/Sponsor — it's the differentiator, it belongs on the card.
