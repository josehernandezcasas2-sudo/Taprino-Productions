How it'd work

Same component, two layoutsThe rail is the existing tab bar (MobileTabBar) with a wide-screen layout, so the groups, the admin tab, the unread dot and the "+" button stay in one place in the code. Below 1180px it's the bottom pill as today.
Menus fly out to the rightClick a group and its menu opens beside the button, anchored to it, and closes on a click elsewhere or on navigation, the same rules as the drop-ups.
Header staysThe top header keeps doing what it does: logo, Connect/Stream links, search, Tapa +, account menu. The rail is the quick-jump between the four groups, so there's some overlap. That's the trade-off to decide on.
Players get out of the wayOn the episode, live and vertical pages the rail should hide when the player goes fullscreen, and the vertical Discover page (its own full-screen takeover) wouldn't show it at all, matching the phone.
