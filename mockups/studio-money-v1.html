Studio money, v1: what leaves Stripe, what stays, and why

The Creator Share page (/admin/creator-money, built) moves money to creators. This page moves money to the studio's bank, on a schedule, by a calculation you can read line by line: the Stripe balance, minus everything that has to stay in Stripe (what creators are owed, advertisers' unused credits, a refund reserve, and the amounts you earmark for films), equals what can leave. Every number has a source, every difference between Stripe and our records is shown, and the studio's own share for upkeep and projects is worked out from the same figures. Example numbers throughout, from a plausible November.

One rule that makes all of it work: the platform's Stripe account must be on manual payouts (Stripe Dashboard → Settings → Payouts). Today it sweeps the balance to the bank on its own, so there would be nothing left for the creator batch on the 1st and nothing for this page to decide. Stripe doesn't let a platform change its own schedule through the API, so this is a one-time setting you make; the page checks it and says so.

3. The month, in order

why the 5th
1st · 09:00 UTCCreator Share batch (built). Transfers from the balance to each creator's Stripe account.
1st – 4thRefunds, disputes and failed transfers from the batch settle. Pending card money from late in the month clears.
5thStudio sweep. The page proposes the amount; in confirm mode you press the button, in automatic mode the cron sends it and emails you the breakdown.
Any dayEarmarks added or released; the reserve rule changed; a discrepancy explained. The calculation updates live.
JanuaryTax forms for creators through Stripe (section 7). The sweep itself never changes.

Settings on the page (full admins): sweep day (5th), mode (confirm now, automatic once you trust it), reserve rule (percent, window, floor), tolerance for unexplained differences ($25 or 1% of the period, whichever is larger; over it the sweep waits for a human). All in site_settings.

4. Earmarks: money for films that stays in Stripe on purpose

"how much stays for the films"

An earmark is a named amount of the studio's own money that the sweep must leave in Stripe. It doesn't move anything and it doesn't change what creators are owed; it just lowers what can leave. When the film is paid for (or the match is spent) you release it.

8. Plumbing

what gets built
Stripe calls (all read, one write)
  • balance.retrieve() for available and pending.
  • accounts.retrieve() for the platform's own payout schedule; the page refuses to sweep unless it reads manual.
  • balanceTransactions.list({ created: since the last sweep }), paged, grouped by reporting_category and matched to our rows by charge id (member_payments, pitch_donations, location_requests, ad top-ups) and transfer id (creator_payouts). What doesn't match is the discrepancy.
  • payouts.create({ amount, currency:'usd', description:'Studio sweep 2026-11' }) with an idempotency key per sweep row. payout.paid / payout.failed on the account webhook endpoint update the row (two more events for the Payments checklist).
  • payouts.list() so a payout made by hand in the Dashboard still shows in the history.
Ours
  • Migration 089: studio_sweeps (cents, stripe_payout_id, status, the frozen breakdown as jsonb, discrepancy, note, created_by, arrived_on), studio_earmarks (label, cents, note, created_by, released_at), studio_adjustments (period, cents, explanation, created_by); settings columns for the day, mode, reserve rule and tolerance.
  • lib/studioMoney.js: getMoneyPage() (balance, schedule check, obligations, reconciliation, studio share), createSweep(), earmark and adjustment writes; the pure arithmetic in lib/studioMoneyRules.js with tests.
  • Pages: /admin/money (this page), API /api/admin/money (GET page, POST sweep / earmark / release / explain), cron /api/cron/studio-sweep on the sweep day (automatic mode only; in confirm mode it emails "sweep ready: $X" and does nothing).
  • Nav: the Money group; audit-log entries for every sweep, earmark and explanation.
  • Not in v1: top-ups from the bank into Stripe (Stripe supports it; add when a batch ever waits on balance), multi-currency, and splitting the studio share by business line (Studio Tapa TV only; the shop and Post Puppeteers don't run through this account).
