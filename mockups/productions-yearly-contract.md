## A. Yearly Stream plans (one builder: server, website, app)

**Prices**: each Stream product (Standard, Premium, Sponsor — `lib/membershipPlans.js`) may have a second active recurring Price with `interval: 'year'`; Jose adds them in Stripe ($79 / $129 / $69 = 10 months, rounded down to the dollar). Nothing is created by code.

1. **Plans** (`lib/membershipPlans.js`, `lib/membershipTiers.js`): each plan carries `monthly` and `yearly` (`{ priceId, amountCents }` or null); the existing flat fields stay the monthly price so older app builds keep working. The fallback (`STRIPE_PRICE_ID`) has no yearly.
2. **Checkout** (`/api/create-checkout-session`): takes `interval: 'month' | 'year'` (default month); refuses `year` for a tier without a yearly price (400 with a line). Everything else as today (tax, metadata, return pages, `appReturn`).
3. **The picker** (`components/MembershipPlans.js`, `mobile/src/components/MembershipPlans.js`): a Monthly / Yearly switch (the plans canvas's look: a two-option pill, "Yearly · 2 months free"), shown only when at least one tier has a yearly price; on Yearly each card shows "$79 a year", "$6.58 a month · 2 months free", and its button checks out yearly; a tier with no yearly price is greyed with a line. A member's plan row (`/account/membership`, the app's Membership screen) says "Renews <date> · yearly" or "· monthly". Plan switching stays Stripe's Customer Portal.
4. **The creator share, month by month** (`lib/creatorShare.js`, `lib/creatorShareRules.js`, the cron `/api/cron/creator-reminders`):
   - `recordMembershipInvoice`: an invoice whose subscription line is a yearly price (read the line's `price.recurring.interval`; a proration-only invoice credits nothing new and is logged) writes its `member_payments` row with `months: 12`, `months_credited: 1`, `next_month_at` = paid_at + 1 calendar month, and credits **month 1** exactly as a monthly invoice does (the tier's monthly share, split by picks → watching → pool, plus the referral bonus while the bonus window lasts).
   - A new daily cron step `creditYearlyMonths()` (after the existing steps, each step runs whatever the others did): for each payment with `next_month_at <= now` and no `stopped_at`, credits the next month — the same split, computed **that day** from the member's lineup or watching then (so changing who they support changes the next month), the bonus by `referralEarnsBonus` on that month's date — then `months_credited + 1` and `next_month_at` + 1 month (null after 12). Claim the row first (update guarded on the `months_credited` read) so two runs never credit a month twice; ledger line ids stable per (payment, month index) through `lib/ledgerIds.js`. At most a few hundred a run; the rest wait for the next.
   - Refund or dispute of a yearly payment (`charge.refunded`, `charge.dispute.created`): sets `stopped_at` and `next_month_at = null` (no further months), and reverses the credited-but-unpaid lines as today for a full refund or a dispute; a partial refund stops the future months only. A won dispute restores the reversed lines as today and does **not** restart the months. A cancelled subscription (`cancel_at_period_end`) keeps crediting through the paid year.
   - Studio money (`lib/studioMoneyRules.js` `canLeaveCents`, `lib/studioMoney.js`): what has to stay adds `yearlySharesAheadCents` = for every running yearly payment, the months not yet credited × its monthly share (+ bonus months still in the window); shown on `/admin/money` with the other obligations and frozen into a sweep's breakdown.
   - The member's own Artist Support page says on a yearly plan: "Your share goes out month by month. Change your picks any time; the next month follows them." (in the rules file).
5. **Terms** (`pages/terms.js`): the membership section says monthly **or yearly** (a yearly plan costs 10 months' price, renews each year, and refunds follow Stripe's portal as today); section 4/5's share sentence covers yearly: the share is credited for each of the 12 months, month by month, split by your picks that month. `TEXT_UPDATED` 2026-11-19. Add the question to `legal/creator-share-terms-draft.md`.

## B. Productions from pitches and the production dashboard — server, shared rules, website (one builder)

1. **Who manages a production**: its owner (the pitch's creator by default) and anyone on its crew list whose role is a producer (`/producer/i`: Producer, Executive producer, Line producer, Co-producer…), plus full admins. Put the rule in the shared rules file (`canManageProduction({ ownerId, members }, viewerId)`) and use it in `lib/productions.js` for the day-to-day writes (status prep/shooting/post/released, crew add/remove/worked, days, notes) and for posting a call or a location request under the production (`lib/productionLinks.js` / wherever `ownedProduction` lives). Only the owner (and full admins) change visibility, archive/remove, the release link, the pitch link. A producer can't remove the owner or themselves from being the owner.
2. **Start the production from a pitch**: `POST /api/pitches/[id]/production { title, logline, kind, startsOn, endsOn, place, tellBackers }` → `startProductionFromPitch(userId, pitchId, input)`: the pitch must be the caller's (`pitches.submitted_by`), `approved`, and have no live production (`productions_one_per_pitch_uq`; a 23505 or the pre-check → 409 with the existing production's id); creates the production with `project_type 'pitch'`, `project_id`, `project_title`, visibility public; `tellBackers` posts a pitch update "<title> is now in production" through the existing pitch-update path (its limits). 10 a day per account. Funding is untouched: backing stays open as it was.
3. **The pitch page** (`lib/pitchDetailHub.js`, `pages/pitches/[id].js`): `production` = the linked live production when the viewer may see it (public, or crew/private for those on it) → `{ id, title, status, statusLabel, startsOn, endsOn, placeLabel, crewCount, openRoles }`; the PitchNow board: an "● IN PRODUCTION" pill on the art, the card "Shooting Dec 6 – 9 in Echo Park · Prep · 4 crew on it · 2 roles open · The production ›" above the Backing block; **Crew needed** lists the production's open calls together with any posted for the pitch. The owner (and a listed producer) sees **Start the production** on their own pitch page when there is none, or **Open its dashboard** when there is.
4. **Your pitches** (`pages/creator/pitch/dashboard.js` — the Dashboard's Pitches tab): each approved pitch shows the "Ready to shoot it?" box with **Start the production** (the StartSheet: a bottom sheet under 900px, a dialog from 900px, prefilled from the pitch — title, logline, kind from the pitch's tags when it maps, dates and place empty; "Tell my <n> backers" switch on), or "In production · Open its dashboard ›".
5. **The production dashboard** `/productions/[id]/dashboard` (owner, listed producers, full admins; anyone else → the production page): the DashPhone and DashLaptop boards — head (‹ Your productions, kicker, title, the status pill as a select, dates · place · visibility · "grew out of the pitch …"), the actions (Post a call → `/crew/calls/new?production=`, Add a day, Book a place → `/locations`, Message crew, Edit), tabs Overview · Crew · Calls · Days & call sheets · Money · Release & distribution (Overview first; each tab its section in full; `?tab=`), four numbers (Raised from the pitch's completed backings and goal, Crew booked and roles open, Next shoot day with call time, Crew pay held), **Needs you** (answers waiting on a call, days with no place, a booked person whose secured pay isn't funded, backers not updated in 14+ days, crew to confirm "worked it" after the last day, a location request waiting on you or unpaid; each with its one link), Funding (from the pitch: bar, raised of goal, what reaches the creator after the cut, latest backers by display name only where they're shown today, Post an update, The pitch page; a production with no pitch shows "Not tied to a pitch" with nothing to start), Calls (answers waiting / answers, closes), Crew by department with pay state (held / not funded / released), Shoot days with place state, Places (the production's location requests with their state), Release & distribution (checklist count, screeners, the Distribution page).
