# Taprino Productions — Studio Tapa TV

Indie OTT streaming platform (studiotapatv.site) for Studio Tapa / Studio Taprino content: films, series, podcasts, short-form vertical video ("Snippets"). Built and run solo by Jose under an LLC that also runs Studio Tapa (Shopify stickers/paper goods) and Post Puppeteers (client video production). Not launched publicly yet.

Differentiator: creators get funded by their audience — **Pitch Room** (donation-style funding, platform takes a cut) and a self-serve **advertiser system** with admin-reviewed ad creative.

Repo: https://github.com/josehernandezcasas2-sudo/Taprino-Productions (branch `main`, Vercel auto-deploys on push).

## Stack

**Membership tiers** (Jose, 2026-10-12): Basic (free, ads, no premium content) / Standard $7.99 (ads, premium content) / Premium $12.99 (ad-free, everything) / Sponsor $6.99 (= Premium, launch-window only, "Sponsor" badge). Paid tiers are Stripe Products with metadata `tier` = `standard|premium|sponsor` + one recurring Price; `lib/membershipPlans.js` lists them live (cached 5 min; falls back to `STRIPE_PRICE_ID` as a lone "Premium" until the products exist), `lib/membershipTiers.js` is the client-safe copy/perks/rank. `getAccountContext` derives `tier` from the active subscription's product (no metadata = premium, so pre-tier members lose nothing; team/comped/promo = premium) and exposes `adFree`; `isSubscriber` still means "any paid tier" and gates premium content; players read `adFree` (live channels ignore it).
